📜 The Lore of Reach Of Vespers

Dive into the mysterious story of a dying world...

🌌The Summoning

In his room, Soren finds a book with a blank cover and empty pages. Curious, he opens it. One blink later, he is thrown into another world, dark and corrupted by a nightmarish magic. Without knowing it, he just answered the call of this dying world, which summoned him as the Chosen One, the only blade capable of cutting through the darkness and restoring the light.

His journey begins at the foot of a massive volcano spitting threatening ashes. With no other choice but to move forward, Soren starts a dangerous climb, pushing back the magma monsters blocking his way. But at the top, a pure lava entity sweeps him aside effortlessly. Defeated, he is thrown into the depths of the world. That is where his true quest begins.

To survive, Soren must grow stronger. He gets his hands on swords forged by fallen heroes and absorbs mystical orbs that boost his vitality and give him new agility. Double jump, dash, he gradually gains all the tools to face whatever this world throws at him.

🗺️The Three Corrupted Zones

Making it back to the surface means crossing three territories rotted through by corruption:

  • The Toxic Swamp: a suffocating bog full of corrupted trees, from which Soren can only escape by taking down the Toxic Soul.
  • The Fungal Caverns: stifling caves that serve as a den for hostile witches, guarded by the Bewitched Soul, master of illusions.
  • The Devastated City: the ruins of a once-glorious civilization, now overrun by sorcerers consumed by the nightmare.

👹Back to the Summit

Once the three zones are purged, Soren returns to the volcano. But he is no longer the same as when he arrived. The magma monsters have mutated, fiercer than ever, as if they can sense the threat he has become. He descends to the bottom of the crater and faces the lava entity once more. This time, the fight turns in his favor, and his victory shatters the very heart of the corruption.

A blinding light swallows the darkness. Soren opens his eyes back in his room. The clock has not moved a second. His memories are hazy, almost dreamlike. On his desk, the book is still there, but it is no longer blank: it now bears the title "Reach Of Vespers", and its pages are filled with the account of the adventure he just lived through.
